PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy deserves clarity.

How VORVAN handles verified customer contact profiles, manual loan information enquiries, institutional enquiries and educational demos. Professional legal review remains pending.

Website scope: Email-verified customer accounts, contact-profile storage and non-binding manual loan information enquiries are now supported. Live CIBIL/bureau retrieval, KYC upload, payment checkout, lender offers and loan applications remain disabled.

1. Information collected

When you voluntarily register through the customer portal, we process a verified email address, name, self-reported Indian mobile number, employment category and short work/occupation description; we record the version and time of your contact/enquiry consent. If you submit a loan information enquiry, we save the selected category, topic, short message, timestamp, manual status and any response. This enables authorised VORVAN staff to review and respond to your request. We do not request PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements, income amounts, KYC documents or actual CIBIL scores. The separate website Guide accepts short informational questions and does not store chat history in our application database.

2. Infrastructure and logs

GitHub Pages and Supabase may process ordinary technical traffic information, IP addresses and infrastructure security logs according to their respective policies. The public site has no intentionally installed third-party advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

3. Domain emails

Clicking an email link opens your mail application. Correspondence is handled by the company's mailbox provider. Please send only necessary information, never sensitive financial records to a general mailbox.

4. Website assistant

The VORVAN Guide is currently a live server-backed rules-based informational assistant, not a generative AI model or financial adviser. It attempts to reject obvious sensitive identifiers but you should avoid sharing any personal information.

5. Admin operations

Customer profiles and information enquiries are stored in Supabase with email-based authentication, row-level ownership security and restricted access. Customers may view their own profile and enquiries. The designated company Owner may review submitted profiles and manually update enquiry status through a separately authenticated Admin area requiring multifactor verification. Customer records are not visible on public pages.

6. Future services

The sample CIBIL-style score, CIBIL Guide plan selector and 30/60/90-day learning steps are fictional and are not stored as credit bureau records. A manual enquiry does not reach a lender and is not a loan application. Any future real credit-score retrieval, lender sharing/application, KYC, payment or model-driven financial analysis requires distinct approved disclosures, permissions, legal basis, authentication and data safeguards.

7. Contact and updates

To request access, correction or deletion of contact/enquiry information, contact legal@vorvan.co.in; retention and any legal exceptions will be reviewed before action. We use contact data for the requested enquiry follow-up and do not automatically share it with lenders or sell it to marketers. Support: support@vorvan.co.in. This notice is a draft subject to professional legal review. Updated 29 October 2026.

INSTITUTIONAL INQUIRIES

Bank / NBFC partnership enquiry data

When an institutional representative voluntarily submits the direct LSP/API inquiry form, we collect their institution name/type, company website, professional name/designation/email, optional business phone, proposed partnership scope, brief message, submission time and selected outreach source. A pseudonymised request fingerprint is used for basic abuse prevention. Please do not submit borrower/customer data, national IDs, financial statements or credentials.

The purpose is to review institutional interest, verify the sender, respond to the enquiry and maintain a limited internal correspondence trail. Data is stored using the VORVAN project's Supabase infrastructure and accessible through restricted, MFA-protected administrative systems; Supabase acts as a technical processing provider. We do not provide public access to submissions or treat submission as approval/contracting.

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably required for contact, compliance and dispute-handling, subject to legal review. Automated deletion has not yet been configured. Contact legal@vorvan.co.in to request correction, access or deletion as applicable.

This notice describes the institutional intake only, not a live consumer lending or credit-reporting service. VORVAN PRIVATE LIMITED is registered; regulated financial services are not live.

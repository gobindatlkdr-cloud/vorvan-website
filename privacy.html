PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy deserves clarity.

How this informational website handles website enquiries and an interactive information guide. Draft pending professional legal review.

Website scope: No customer account, live CIBIL/bureau API, KYC form, payment checkout or loan application exists on this public website.

1. Information collected

The public site does not ask visitors to upload identity documents, credit reports, bank statements or personal financial information. The VORVAN Guide receives short questions for informational answers and does not store conversation history in an application database.

2. Infrastructure and logs

GitHub Pages and Supabase may process ordinary technical traffic information, IP addresses and infrastructure security logs according to their respective policies. The public site has no intentionally installed third-party advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

3. Domain emails

Clicking an email link opens your mail application. Correspondence is handled by the company's mailbox provider. Please send only necessary information, never sensitive financial records to a general mailbox.

4. Website assistant

The VORVAN Guide is currently a live server-backed rules-based informational assistant, not a generative AI model or financial adviser. It attempts to reject obvious sensitive identifiers but you should avoid sharing any personal information.

5. Admin operations

Administrative work tracking uses separate Supabase authentication, server-side access rules and multifactor controls. Admin records are not publicly accessible from this website.

6. Future services

Any real credit-score retrieval, lender application, KYC, payment or model-driven financial analysis would need separate disclosures, approvals, permissions, data safeguards and lawful consent arrangements before deployment.

7. Contact and updates

Privacy or legal queries: legal@vorvan.co.in. Support: support@vorvan.co.in. This notice is a draft subject to professional legal review. Updated 29 October 2026.

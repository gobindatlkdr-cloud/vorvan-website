PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy should be understood, not hidden.

This is a draft privacy notice for VORVAN’s informational website. It is not a substitute for a future mobile app or digital lending privacy policy.

Drafted: 28 October 2026 · For legal and operational review before production use

Before public launch: Confirm the legal entity, registered address, data controller details, privacy contact, vendor arrangements, retention periods and applicable law with professional review.

1. Scope

This notice relates only to this informational website. It does not cover any proposed credit-score application, digital lending journey, KYC workflow, banking or bureau integration, none of which are offered through this website.

2. Information you choose to share

This website does not provide a customer registration form or collect financial documents. If you choose to email us, we receive the information in your message, which may include your name, contact details and the substance of your enquiry.

3. Why we use enquiries

We may use correspondence to respond to your enquiry, assess a potential business collaboration, keep relevant business records and address website-related concerns, subject to applicable law.

4. Website hosting and technical data

When hosted, the infrastructure provider may process IP addresses, request logs and related technical information necessary for website delivery, security and abuse prevention. Its practices depend on the selected hosting provider and configuration.

5. Cookies and analytics

The supplied website template does not include advertising trackers, third-party analytics scripts, embedded social feeds or first-party tracking cookies. This statement must be reassessed if services are added.

6. Sharing and security

Website enquiry correspondence is handled through the configured email service. Technical and operational safeguards depend on the final account settings and providers. No technology can promise absolute security.

7. Retention and your requests

Retention and request-handling procedures must be confirmed before production publication. To ask about an enquiry you sent, email vorvan.pvt.ltd@gmail.com.

8. Updates

This notice will be updated when website operations, service providers or applicable requirements change. Future credit-bureau and regulated digital-lending services would need additional, specific privacy disclosures, consent mechanisms and compliance review.

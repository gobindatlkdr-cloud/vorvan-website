Direct LSP partnerships and lending API integration for banks and NBFCs.

TWO DIRECT ROUTES

Different discussions, one institutional focus.

VORVAN seeks direct lender-facing technology collaboration rather than DSA-only, agent-only or loan-referral arrangements. Neither route is currently an active production relationship.

Formal direct LSP partnership

Explore a documented technology service relationship with a regulated lending institution. A discussion would cover the intended product journey, permitted activities, customer disclosures, consent handling, lender oversight, grievance escalation, vendor reviews and operating responsibilities.

The lender retains its lending decisions and its own regulatory obligations. No customer journey or activity is activated simply by submitting an inquiry.

Direct lending API integration

Explore sandbox feasibility, published API specifications, authentication, product and eligibility fields, application handoff, secure status callbacks, reconciliation and test plans. Technical access would require the institution's approval and suitable contracts.

VORVAN does not currently claim working connections to any bank or NBFC. All integration capabilities described here are proposed or under evaluation.

TECHNICAL & RISK REVIEW

What an institutional technology team may evaluate.

These are review subjects for a possible future integration, not a list of deployed production endpoints.

Authentication and access

API credentials, environment separation, least-privilege permissions, encryption, supported authentication mechanisms, key rotation and logging controls.

Loan application handoff

Customer-facing lender identification, approved field mapping, consent boundaries, application references, lender-owned decisions and journey transitions.

Callbacks and resilience

Event notifications, status mapping, deduplication, retry rules, exception handling and technical monitoring responsibilities.

Customer data protection

Data minimisation, encryption, retention, data access controls, auditability and response procedures appropriate to the agreed scope.

Operational ownership

Service levels, incident contacts, support escalation, reconciliation, dispute handling and responsibility for change management.

Formal compliance review

Entity diligence, contractual allocation of tasks, required customer disclosures, legal approvals and applicable RBI/lender controls before launch.

ENGAGEMENT PROCESS

From initial contact to a documented decision.

STEP 01

Introduce institution

Send the official website, authorised work email, intended direct LSP or API model and a short business agenda.

STEP 02

Review product fit

Discuss credit journey design, lender ownership, target product, permitted data and commercial feasibility.

STEP 03

Due diligence

Assess incorporation status, risk and security controls, legal documentation and any available sandbox pathway.

STEP 04

Approvals before activation

Any production launch must follow signed agreements, lender approval, technical testing and relevant compliance checks.

COMMON INSTITUTIONAL QUESTIONS

Answers before an LSP or API call.

Is VORVAN already a registered NBFC or direct lender?

No. This is a developing fintech initiative, and the proposed company is still awaiting incorporation. VORVAN does not claim a lending licence.

Are bank/NBFC APIs currently connected?

No active integration is represented. The website outlines a proposed collaboration model and topics for future technical and regulatory review.

Are DSA or lead-referral enquiries accepted?

This institutional channel is focused on formal direct LSP and lender API discussions, not agent-only or lead-selling arrangements.

Should we share customer KYC or credit information?

No. Only provide limited professional contact and institutional scope details. Do not send borrower Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, account information, credit reports or credentials.

Does an inquiry create a partnership?

No. An inquiry is manually reviewed and does not create a contract, validate an institution or activate customer-facing services.

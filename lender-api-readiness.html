BANK & NBFC TECHNICAL PLANNING

Lender API sandbox and direct LSP readiness checklist.

A practical, non-confidential discussion checklist for regulated lenders considering potential digital credit technology integration with VORVAN.

Prepared as VORVAN planning information · Updated 4 November 2026 · VORVAN PRIVATE LIMITED is registered; no active lender agreements, API credentials, credit bureau access or loan origination service are claimed.

Review the checklist ↗Contact institutional desk
01 · PARTNERSHIP SCOPE

Agree what is being explored before requesting a sandbox.

Technical credentials alone do not establish a formal Lending Service Provider relationship. An institution's partnership, technology, risk and compliance teams would first need to define the permitted scope.

Business scope checklist

  • Bank or NBFC legal name, official domain and authorised counterpart
  • Direct LSP relationship, direct API feasibility, or both
  • Product type, customer geography and proposed journey boundary
  • Who displays lender identity, pricing, terms and disclosures
  • Who makes credit decisions and who owns customer support
  • Any vendor due-diligence and contracting prerequisites

Minimum safe information to exchange

  • Official institution contact details and broad business agenda
  • Public API documentation or approved non-production specifications
  • High-level expected authentication and consent requirements
  • Intended sandbox test window and review participants
  • Public or approved policy references

Do not upload borrower KYC, PAN, Aadhaar, OTP, banking statements, credit reports, production keys or private client credentials to the website.

02 · API BOUNDARIES

Confirm the technical contract before writing integration code.

These questions help both teams identify mismatched assumptions. The exact answers depend on a lender's published interface and written authorisation.

Environment & authentication

  • Distinct sandbox and production endpoints
  • Token issuance and expiry rules
  • Allowed client authentication mechanism
  • Credential custody and rotation ownership
  • Network restrictions and IP allowlist approach

Product & application fields

  • Required versus optional data fields
  • Loan product identifiers and supported eligibility inputs
  • Consent reference and disclosure version identifiers
  • Request validation and error response formats
  • Institution-owned loan decision and approval steps

Events & lifecycle

  • Unique application and correlation identifiers
  • Documented status event names and ordering rules
  • Webhook signing and replay protection
  • Retries, duplicate events and idempotent handling
  • Exception workflow and reconciliation ownership
03 · SANDBOX ACCEPTANCE

Example non-production test scenarios.

The following are proposed test cases, not endpoints or integrations currently provided by VORVAN. Lenders should supply approved fictional test records for their own sandbox.

TEST 01

Authentication failure

Expired or missing test token must be rejected without disclosing secrets; verify expected error codes and safe logging.

TEST 02

Invalid application

Missing mandatory fields or invalid product identifier must result in a documented response. No live borrower data is used.

TEST 03

Duplicate submission

Replay a request with the same idempotency key; confirm the agreed handling without accidental duplicate application creation.

TEST 04

Callback retry

Send duplicate, delayed and out-of-order fictional status events; verify signature validation, safe retry and reconciliation behavior.

Additional negative tests

  • Wrong environment and unapproved origin
  • Malformed callback signature
  • Consent reference missing or outside approved scope
  • Rate limit exceeded and temporary downstream outage
  • Interrupted network response with a safe retry

Evidence to capture

  • Test case ID and sandbox date
  • Expected result versus observed result
  • Redacted correlation ID, never customer PII
  • Defect owner, severity and retest disposition
  • Joint sign-off of the agreed acceptance criteria
04 · GOVERNANCE

Security and compliance gates before any production conversation.

Institution-specific legal reviews and applicable regulatory requirements must be considered by qualified teams. This checklist is not legal advice or a claim of regulatory approval.

Customer and data protections

Define approved data categories, purpose and consent; storage locations, access permissions, encryption, retention, breach response and deletion responsibilities.

Customer-facing accountability

Identify the regulated lender to users, clarify pricing and disclosures, assign grievance handling and document responsibility for customer support and disputes.

Institutional review

Verify legal entity readiness, intended vendor role, security reviews, proposed outsourcing terms, commercial decision-makers and required written approvals.

05 · DECISION RECORD

What a practical next-step note should contain.

For an initial meeting, record non-sensitive decisions in a short shared agenda. This avoids confusing a positive discussion with technical or regulatory approval.

Meeting outcome template

  • Institution and official counterpart
  • Direct relationship route and proposed product
  • Public documentation or authorised sandbox next step
  • Named team owner for technical, legal and risk items
  • Open dependencies and review date
  • Explicit outcome: exploratory / on hold / declined / approved subject to contract

Do not assume these outcomes

  • A demonstration is not a live lender connection
  • A sandbox invitation is not production approval
  • An email discussion is not an executed LSP contract
  • Technical readiness does not replace regulatory review
  • Submitting the public form does not verify a bank/NBFC or create privileged access

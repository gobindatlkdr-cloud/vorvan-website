FOR REGULATED LENDERS

Built for collaboration, not shortcuts.

We are seeking direct technology and formal LSP partnership conversations with regulated lenders—never presenting lead referral or DSA-only arrangements as our core model.

Direct APIsFormal LSPSandbox evaluationCustomer safeguards
Trust is foundational

Clarity at every stage.

The platform is in development. Trust begins by explaining what exists now and what needs partner authorisation before activation.

  • No lender or bureau approval is claimed without a signed agreement.
  • Actual credit pulls require authorised access and explicit customer consent.
  • Loan pricing, eligibility, approval and disbursal belong to the relevant regulated lender.
  • Security and privacy controls must be verified before any live customer data is processed.

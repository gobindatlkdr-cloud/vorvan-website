PARTNER VALUE PROPOSITION

A focused technology conversation—not a lead-selling arrangement.

VORVAN is building toward a digital credit journey that can be assessed by regulated lending partners. We are seeking formal direct relationships, with technical, regulatory and commercial details agreed before any activation.

02 · INTEGRATION

Structured lender API approach

03 · GOVERNANCE

Responsibilities defined upfront

Agree data handling, customer disclosures, grievance routing, security evaluation, auditability, service levels and oversight within a formal contract.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before an institutional discussion.

Is VORVAN acting as an NBFC or lending from its own books?

No. This corporate website does not represent VORVAN as an RBI-regulated lender. Our focus is exploring formal technology/LSP arrangements with regulated lenders.

Does VORVAN offer DSA-only or lead referral partnerships?

No. Our focus is direct LSP agreements and lender API integration discussions, not agent-only lead distribution.

Is API or sandbox access already enabled?

No. Integration modules are shown as planned and are disabled until due diligence, agreements, security and technical approval are complete.

What information should an institution send?

Institution name, work email, type of proposed direct relationship and a short scope note. Please do not send borrowers' personal or financial information.

How can we request a discussion?

Write to partnerships@vorvan.co.in. The VORVAN Guide also answers common process questions.

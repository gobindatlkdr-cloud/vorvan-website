WHAT WE ARE SEEKING

Collaborate where the fit is real.

Our priority is direct integration with a regulated lender or a formal relationship that enables a compliant digital lending flow.

  • India-based regulated banks and NBFCs with digital lending programmes
  • Direct API or official LSP onboarding teams

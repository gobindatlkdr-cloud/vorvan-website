FIRST DISCUSSION AGENDA

A focused introduction, not a complicated application.

The first exchange can establish whether a formal lender conversation is worthwhile before confidential documents or a sandbox are considered.

01

Institutional fit

Identify the bank or NBFC, authorised business counterpart, eligible product area and potential partnership path.

02

Technology scope

Agree on the high-level borrower journey, direct API feasibility and which teams would own the interface review.

03

Controls and approval

Identify legal entity prerequisites, security standards, compliance reviews, risk ownership and formal contracting gates.

04

Next step

Record the authorised contact, review owner and whether an exploratory meeting or further documentation is appropriate.

CLEAR AND RESPONSIBLE

Information we can discuss safely.

For an initial enquiry, provide an institutional name, official website, business contact and a non-confidential description of direct LSP/API requirements.

Suitable for a first enquiry

  • Bank or regulated NBFC business type and official domain
  • Partnership or digital lending team designation
  • Interest: direct LSP, direct lender API or both
  • High-level use case and preferred follow-up method

Do not submit

  • Customer PAN, Aadhaar, OTP, KYC or credit reports
  • Customer banking statements or confidential files
  • Production API keys, access tokens or passwords
  • Unapproved financial or borrower information

## Interpretation (use actual measured queries)
- Impressions but low CTR: review whether the title accurately answers the query; compare query to the landing-page intent; do not promise an arbitrary CTR gain.
- No impressions on a new page: verify access, indexing status, internal link and content usefulness before making more SEO pages.
- Queries from consumers looking for personal loans rather than institutional API work: preserve institutional wording and avoid promoting public loan application services.
- Relevant impressions and inbound institutional contact: prioritize the landing-page use-case that actually attracts bank/NBFC teams.
- Unrelated queries: avoid keyword stuffing and false claims.

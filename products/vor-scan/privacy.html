Vor Scan · Android document scanner

Privacy Policy

This policy applies only to Vor Scan, an Android document scanner and PDF tool published by VORVAN PRIVATE LIMITED. It does not govern the company's other websites, lending-related services or future apps.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

At a glance: Vor Scan lets you scan, edit, save and share documents on your device without creating an account. The app uses Google Play services for camera scanning and may use Google AdMob for advertising. These third-party components process some technical data as described below.

1. Documents, photos and PDFs

Vor Scan processes document pages captured by its scanner and the photos or PDFs that you choose to import. It keeps working pages, document names, dates and filter previews in the app's private storage on your device. If you import a PDF, the app may also keep an app-private copy of that source PDF.

The current document workflow is designed to work locally. Vor Scan does not require a user account and does not upload the contents of your scanned documents to a VORVAN-operated document server as part of creating, editing or exporting scans.

2. Camera scanner and Google ML Kit

Camera scanning uses the Google ML Kit document scanner through Google Play services. Google states that document image processing happens on the device; scanning resources may need to download before first use. Google may process technical and diagnostic information about the device, app, scanner settings, performance and feature use. See Google's document scanner documentation and ML Kit data disclosure.

3. Saving and sharing

When you choose to save a PDF or JPG, Android places the exported file at the location you select. When you choose to share a file, Android passes it to the receiving app or service you choose. That recipient handles its copy under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not automatically send your documents to another person or company.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

Versions of Vor Scan configured to display advertisements use Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). For ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention, Google's SDK may collect and share information such as IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics and advertising or device identifiers. Google states that the SDK encrypts the data it transfers. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform may present consent or privacy-choice controls. When an “Ad privacy choices” option is available in your version of Vor Scan, use it to revisit your preferences. You can also manage advertising identifiers in Android device settings.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Working documents remain in app-private storage until you delete them from My Scans or remove the app's data. To remove a stored document, open it in My Scans and choose Delete. Temporary export or sharing files may remain in the app cache until cleared by the app or Android.

Files saved elsewhere on the phone, shared with other apps or retained in a device backup must be deleted separately from those places. Android backup or device-transfer features may preserve copies depending on your device settings.

6. Access and security

Vor Scan needs access only to the content you select or capture for scanning and exporting. Camera scanning takes place in Google's document-scanner interface. Protect your device and exported documents using Android's available security controls. No software can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Vor Scan's functionality, advertising configuration or data practices change. The effective or last-updated date on this page will be revised when changes are published. Privacy policies for other VORVAN products are maintained separately.

8. Support and privacy requests

For questions about Vor Scan, this policy or privacy-related requests, contact support@vorvan.co.in. Since the current document library is stored on your device rather than in a VORVAN document account, you can remove local scans through the app or Android settings; we cannot remotely erase copies you saved or shared with other services.

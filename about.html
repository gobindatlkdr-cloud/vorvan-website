ABOUT VORVAN

Built in India. Focused on financial clarity.

VORVAN is an emerging fintech technology initiative based in West Bengal, India, with a vision for accessible credit education and responsible financial connectivity.

OUR PURPOSE

Make credit easier to understand—and decisions easier to evaluate.

Financial information can be intimidating. Our product direction combines clear explanations, guided credit-health education and potential connections to regulated lenders through formal partner channels.

We want people to understand what their credit information means before making important financial decisions.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Clarity. Consent. Accountability.

We prefer accurate disclosures over exaggerated promises and approved technology paths over shortcuts.

  • Respect users and their data
  • Be clear about what is available
  • Work with appropriately authorised providers
  • Build for transparent customer experiences
